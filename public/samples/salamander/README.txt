Grand piano samples used by the built-in "Grand Piano" sound.

Salamander Grand Piano (V3) recorded by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). This is the compact 30-note re-encoding
distributed with the Tone.js project's audio examples (https://github.com/Tonejs/audio),
fetched from https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/ . One sample every three semitones
from A0 (MIDI 21) to C8 (MIDI 108); files are named by note with "s" for sharp
(Ds1 = D#1). Other pitches are made by playing the nearest sample faster or slower.
