# Progressive commercial auto liability reserve range

Units are $ millions.

The analysis uses a Bornhuetter-Ferguson framework:

`Ultimate = latest incurred + unreported percentage × expected ultimate`

Expected ultimate is based on Commercial Lines net earned premium, an 85%-95% allocation of that premium to liability, and accident-year expected loss-ratio ranges. The unreported percentages are calibrated to the latest carried IBNR divided by carried ultimate from the uploaded 2025 10-K triangle.

| AY | Book ultimate | BF low | BF base | BF high |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2021 | 3,585 | 3,586 | 3,589 | 3,591 |
| 2022 | 4,992 | 4,996 | 5,013 | 5,031 |
| 2023 | 5,707 | 5,721 | 5,748 | 5,784 |
| 2024 | 6,018 | 6,017 | 6,074 | 6,135 |
| 2025 | 6,831 | 6,671 | 6,801 | 6,938 |

These are BF ranges, not a full model-risk range. They are intentionally close to carried values for mature accident years because the 10-K shows little remaining IBNR. The 2025 range has more sensitivity because 17.6% of the carried ultimate remains unreported.
